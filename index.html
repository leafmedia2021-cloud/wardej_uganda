01 / Who we are

When women have the power to shape their lives, whole communities flourish.

WARDEJ-Uganda is a feminist organization protecting the holistic rights of women and girls. Our mission is to advance women’s rights through legal empowerment, land justice, reproductive health advocacy and sustainable environmental management. We bridge the gap between national policy and grassroots reality through legal aid, advocacy and community-led development.
